About CartDetox

A safe space for the shopping urge.

Our Story

The urge is real. The solution is CartDetox.

Compulsive shopping is more common than most people realise. The dopamine rush of browsing, the excitement of adding to cart, the satisfaction of "purchasing" — these feelings are powerful and real, even if the purchase isn't.

CartDetox was built as a tool to help people satisfy that urge in a completely harmless way. Browse real-looking fashion. Add items to a real-feeling cart. Complete a real checkout flow. Get a real order confirmation. And walk away with your wallet completely untouched.

We believe that acknowledging the urge is healthier than denying it. So we gave it a home.

How It Works

Simple, by design.

01

Browse & Shop

Explore our curated fashion and lifestyle catalogue. Filter by category, use the search, save what you love.

02

You're Not Alone

Resources & Support

If you feel that compulsive shopping is affecting your finances, relationships, or wellbeing, please know that help is available. CartDetox is a tool, not a treatment.

🧠

Spenders Anonymous

A 12-step fellowship for people with compulsive spending or debting behaviours.

Support Group
💬

Talk to a Therapist

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) is particularly effective for compulsive shopping. Ask your GP for a referral or search for a therapist specialising in behavioural addiction.

Professional Help
📚

To Buy or Not to Buy

A self-help book by April Lane Benson exploring the psychology behind compulsive shopping and practical strategies for change.

Reading
🌱

Minimalism Mindset

Explore minimalism communities online — r/minimalism, The Minimalists podcast, and similar resources offer a supportive, non-judgemental space for reflection.

Community
🌿

CartDetox Rewards

Our built-in mindfulness challenges are designed to redirect the shopping impulse into positive activities. Complete them daily to earn Detox Tokens and build better habits.

In-App
🔁

Financial Therapy Association

The FTA connects people to financial therapists who understand the emotional and psychological dimensions of money and spending.

Professional Help
